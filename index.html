Privacy Policy — Lamplight: Akari Light Up

Effective date: 14 September 2026 Developer: MechanicWB (independent developer) Contact: mechanicwb2@gmail.com Published at: https://mechanicwb2-hub.github.io/lamplight-site/ (source of truth is this file; the site repo mechanicwb2-hub/lamplight-site republishes it)

Lamplight is a logic puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles, what it does not, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Lamplight has no accounts and no server of its own. Your puzzle progress, statistics and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app shows advertising from Google AdMob. Google's advertising SDK may process your advertising identifier, IP address and device information. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom you are asked for consent first.
  • The app offers one in-app purchase (removing ads) through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google; Lamplight only stores whether the purchase is active.
  • Lamplight does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information itself.

Information stored on your device

Lamplight saves the following locally, using Android's app storage, so that you can pick up where you left off:

  • which levels you have finished, and how long and how many moves each took;
  • the board you are in the middle of;
  • your day streak, daily-puzzle history and hint usage;
  • whether the remove-ads purchase is active.

This information never leaves your device through Lamplight. Uninstalling the app deletes it. If you use Android's app backup, it may be included in your device backup under Google's terms.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you have purchased the ad-free version, Lamplight shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier;
  • IP address and coarse, IP-derived location;
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, app version);
  • ad interaction data (which ads were shown and tapped).

How Google uses this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Consent in the EEA and UK. Before any ad is requested, Lamplight shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform. You can decline personalised advertising. You can change your choice later from the app's settings.

Your controls. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising identifier and opt out of ads personalisation in Settings → Google → Ads. Ads are never shown on the daily puzzle, and never during the first session after installing.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The single purchase, "Remove ads", is processed by Google Play. Lamplight never sees your payment details. Google's handling of purchase data is governed by the Google Play Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy. Lamplight stores only the fact that the purchase is active, so that ads stay off, and restores it from Google Play when you reinstall.

Children

Lamplight is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country) and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of personal data about you. Because Lamplight itself holds no personal data, requests about advertising data should be directed to Google through the controls above. For anything else, write to the contact address at the top of this page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
